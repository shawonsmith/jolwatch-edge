# 90-Second Demo Recording Plan

## 0–10 seconds — Problem

“Small schools and clinics may know total water use but cannot quickly identify hidden leaks, tank overflow or how long essential water will last.”

## 10–20 seconds — Solution

Open JolWatch Edge and say: “This offline-first prototype turns simple flow and tank readings into explainable decisions.”

## 20–55 seconds — Guided evidence demo

Press **Guided demo**.

1. Normal: show that the balance is within tolerance.
2. Hidden leak: show the loss percentage and persistence rule.
3. Overflow: show the 98% tank level and overflow alert.
4. Sensor mismatch: show that inconsistent readings are rejected before action.

## 55–72 seconds — Impact workflow

Show the Essential Reserve Clock. Then capture an alert baseline, simulate a repair and press **Verify & save incident**.

## 72–82 seconds — Tank Health

Open Tank Health and show the cleaning/inspection guidance plus the safety disclaimer.

## 82–90 seconds — Honest close

“The current results use simulated data. Our next step is an ESP32 sensor test rig to measure accuracy, false alerts, detection time and verified litres saved.”

## Recording settings

- Resolution: 1920×1080
- Browser zoom: 90–100%
- Keep the mouse movement slow
- Record in a quiet room
- Do not claim real-world savings or completed sensor validation
